Pricing

Last updated 26 July 2026

The mathematics is free and always will be. What costs money is the writing.

Free, permanently

No account, no trial, no card. Everything the engine computes is yours:

  • Your natal chart, at full precision, from the Swiss Ephemeris
  • The sky right now, and the sky on any date you choose
  • Transits and a sixty-day forecast
  • Tarot draws and the natal arcana signature
  • Relationship charts, progressions, returns, eclipses, harmonics, fixed stars
  • Your shelf, your journal, and the ability to export all of it

Most of this also runs inside your browser, so it keeps working with no connection at all. That is not a limitation of the free tier — it is the design.

What unlocking buys

Written and spoken work, composed by a large language model against your actual chart. These cost real money to produce every time they run, which is what the price covers.

TierPriceWhat it opens
Supporter $3 / month In-depth written readings on the cloud model, premium narrated voice, the daily transit reading.
Oracle $9 / month Everything in Supporter, plus the full Oracle Report, the Course, and the deck-art studio.
Deluxe personal report $5 once One Oracle session expanded into a long, research-paper-style personal report, ready to print. Bought per session, not a subscription. Requires Oracle.

These are founding rates. They are deliberately low while the printed edition is still being built, and they will rise later. If you subscribe now, your price stays where it is — a rise applies to new subscriptions, not to yours.

Paying another way

There is also a crypto contribution route for those who prefer it. Any amount unlocks supporter access. Contributions are gifts to the work and cannot be refunded, since the transaction can't be reversed — if you want the protection of the refund policy, pay by card.

The small print, said plainly

  • Monthly tiers renew until you cancel. Cancel any time from inside the app.
  • Cancelling keeps your access until the paid period ends.
  • Fourteen-day refunds — see the refund policy.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Your bank may add a conversion fee.
  • Card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never reach our servers.
