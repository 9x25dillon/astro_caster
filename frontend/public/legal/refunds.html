Refunds

Last updated 26 July 2026

Fourteen days. If Astra isn't what you hoped, write and say so within fourteen days of your payment and you get your money back. You do not have to explain why.

How to ask

Write to the address at the foot of this page with the email address you used at checkout, or the date and amount of the payment. That's enough to find it. We aim to answer within a few days, and the money returns by the same route it arrived — usually a few more days for your bank to show it.

You don't need us to stop a subscription. Cancelling is self-service: open Support inside the app and choose Manage or cancel subscription. That opens Stripe's own portal, where you can cancel, stop auto-renewal or change your card. No email, no waiting on us.

Cancelling keeps your access until the period you have already paid for runs out. It is not the same as a refund — ask for one of those if you want the money back as well.

What the fourteen days apply to

What you boughtRefund
A monthly tier (Supporter or Oracle) Full refund of the most recent payment within 14 days of it being taken. Earlier months are not refunded.
A deluxe personal report Full refund within 14 days — including if the report has already been compiled. If it didn't tell you anything worth having, that is exactly the case this policy is for.
A crypto contribution Contributions to the treasury are gifts to the work and are not refundable, because the transaction cannot be reversed. Please use a card if you want the protection of this policy.

What happens to your access

A refund switches the unlock off. That is automatic: when the refund lands, Stripe tells the observatory and the entitlement is revoked. Anything you saved — charts, shelved readings, journal entries — stays in your browser and is yours to keep. Refunding does not delete your work.

After fourteen days

We are not strict about the boundary. If something went genuinely wrong — the observatory was down, a report never compiled, you were charged twice — write to us whenever you notice, and we will put it right. The fourteen days are the promise, not the limit of our patience.

Your legal rights

This policy is offered in addition to any rights you have under the consumer law where you live, and does not replace them.
