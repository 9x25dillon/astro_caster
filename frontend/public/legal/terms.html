What Astra is

Astra computes real astronomical positions — the genuine sky at a given moment and place — and interprets them through astrological and tarot traditions. The calculations are real astronomy, drawn from the Swiss Ephemeris. The interpretations are a tradition of meaning laid over them.

This is not advice. Nothing Astra produces is medical, psychological, legal or financial advice, and nothing here predicts what will happen to you. It is offered for reflection and enjoyment. Please do not make a decision that matters — about your health, your money, your relationships or anyone's safety — because a chart or a card said so. If you are struggling, talk to a professional who can actually help.

Nothing Astra produces is a life sentence — it is a life poem.
